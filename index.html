Is Capital Edge SEBI registered?

Capital Edge is an educational institution providing stock market knowledge and conceptual training. We are not SEBI-registered as an investment advisor or research analyst. Our programs are designed for educational purposes only, to help learners understand market mechanics and develop disciplined approaches.

Do you provide stock tips or trading calls?

No, we do not provide stock tips, trading calls, or any buy/sell/hold recommendations. Our focus is strictly on teaching general trading principles, risk management, and market concepts for educational purposes, in full compliance with SEBI guidelines.

Who should take this course?

Our courses are designed for beginners and intermediate learners who want to understand stock market concepts, trading principles, and risk management techniques. They are ideal for anyone seeking to build foundational knowledge of trading.

Can I expect profits after this course?

Our courses are purely educational and aim to teach trading concepts. We do not guarantee profits or financial success, as trading involves significant risks and outcomes depend on individual decisions, market conditions, and risk management. Always consult a SEBI-registered advisor for investment decisions.

What is the difference between education and investment advice?

Education involves teaching general concepts, principles, and strategies about the stock market, which we provide. Investment advice involves recommending specific stocks or actions, which we do not offer. For personalized advice, consult a SEBI-registered advisor.

Do I need prior trading knowledge to join?

No prior trading knowledge is required. Our courses are designed for beginners and include foundational lessons to help you understand stock market concepts from scratch.

How do I practice what I learn?

We recommend practicing concepts using paper trading or demo accounts offered by SEBI-registered brokers. These allow you to simulate trading without financial risk, helping you apply what you've learned in a safe environment.

What is your refund policy?

Please note that all course purchases are final and non-refundable. Since our courses provide immediate access to digital educational content, we do not offer refunds once a purchase is completed. We recommend reviewing the course details carefully before enrolling. For any questions prior to purchase, feel free to contact us at capitaledge@example.com.
